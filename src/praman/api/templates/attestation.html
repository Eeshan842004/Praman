Pinned bundle spans

Each span replays against the bundle that actually authorised it. Two spans is what makes this an audit of a policy change, not just of a policy. Ranges cover decision entries only, so consecutive spans do not touch — the entries between them are the actuation and outcome rows belonging to the last decision of the earlier span. Only a decision carries a bundle revision; an actuation is not authorised, the decision behind it was.

Chain

Recomputes sha256(prev_hash || canonical_bytes(row)) over every entry. Proves nothing was changed after the fact. This page runs it live, which is why the banner above is a measurement rather than a label.

Replay

Re-POSTs every stored policy input to OPA loaded with that entry's pinned bundle and compares the verdict. Proves the record was true when it was written — which a hash chain structurally cannot do, because a writer that bypasses the policy engine and records its own verdict still hashes perfectly.

Run it yourself

replay spawns a policy engine per bundle, so it runs from the terminal
./scripts/verify.sh

Honest limit: this is tamper-evident, not tamper-preventing. It detects modification; it does not stop a sufficiently privileged actor from attempting one. praman tamper has to drop the append-only trigger first — a visible act — and the chain still catches it.

Pinned bundle spans

Each span replays against the bundle that actually authorised it. Two spans is what makes this an audit of a policy change, not just of a policy.
Ranges cover decision entries only, so consecutive spans do not touch: the entries between them are the actuation and outcome rows belonging to the last decision of the earlier span. Only a decision carries a bundle revision - an actuation is not authorised, the decision behind it was.

Two independent checks

Chain. Recomputes sha256(prev_hash || canonical_bytes(row)) over every entry. Proves nothing was changed after the fact. This page runs it live, which is why the banner above is a measurement and not a label.

Replay. Re-POSTs every stored policy input to OPA loaded with that entry's pinned bundle and compares the verdict. Proves the record was true when written - which a hash chain structurally cannot do, because a writer that bypasses the policy engine and records its own verdict still hashes perfectly.

Replay spawns a policy engine per bundle, so it runs from the terminal rather than on a page load: ./scripts/verify.sh

Honest limit: this is tamper-evident, not tamper-preventing. It detects modification. It does not stop a sufficiently privileged actor from attempting one - praman tamper has to drop the append-only trigger first, and the chain still catches it.
